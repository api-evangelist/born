# Born

Born (formerly Slay) is a Berlin-based consumer AI company founded in 2022 by Fabian Kamberi (CEO), Jannis Ringwald, and Stefan Quernhorst. Born builds AI characters and companions designed for shared, social experiences. Its flagship product, Pengu, is a co-parented virtual pet with 15M+ registered users; the company is expanding into a multi-character platform ("Friends") and a photorealistic voice-and-video companion experience ("Life"). Born has launched five consumer social apps that reached #1 globally on the App Store, and raised a $15M Series A in September 2025 (Accel, Tencent, Laton Ventures), for ~$25M total funding.

Born is a consumer mobile-app maker with no public developer API, SDK, or documentation surface. This repo therefore carries an identity profile plus a probed domain-security artifact; there are no spec-derived artifacts to enrich.
